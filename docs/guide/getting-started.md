# Getting started

Make a drawing, paint on it, then change one of your strokes after you've painted it. It takes about five minutes.

Keys are written the way a Mac shows them. On Windows and Linux, cmd is ctrl.

## Get klad

[Build klad from source](https://github.com/klad-app/klad#from-source): it is free and takes about ten minutes. Signed builds that install with a double click are planned; [klad.studio](https://klad.studio/pricing) has the list to hear when. On a Mac the pen gives pressure and tilt, on Windows pressure, and on Linux you draw with the mouse for now.

## Make a drawing

![The start window, with recent drawings and the samples](../images/start-window.png)

1. Start klad and press **cmd N**.
2. Type a name and click **Create**.

There's nothing to save: klad writes every change to the drawing as you make it. **cmd S** says so, and where the drawing is.

## Paint, then change a stroke

1. Press **B** and paint a few strokes.
2. Press **S** and click one of them.
3. In the bar next to the selection, click the palette button.
4. Pick a colour on the wheel. The stroke takes the new colour and keeps its shape and pressure.
5. Press **cmd Z** to undo that, and **cmd shift Z** to redo it.

Every stroke stays editable like this, hours later too: see [Changing strokes](selecting.md).

## Find your way around

- **cmd K** finds any command: type a word for what you want to do.
- **?** lists every key.
- Hold **space** and drag to pan, pinch or hold **cmd** and scroll to zoom, and press **F** to see the whole canvas.
- **R** and **shift R** turn the view, and so does a two-finger twist (hold **shift** for steps of 15 degrees). Click **upright** at the top of the canvas, or press **alt R**, to put it back.
- Right click the canvas for copy, paste and **Move to new layer**.

**Help > First steps** in the editor walks you through a shape, a fill and an agent.
